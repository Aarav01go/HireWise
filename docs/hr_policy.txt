# HireWise HR Policy Document

## 1. Working Hours
* Standard working hours are from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Monday to Friday.
* Employees are entitled to a 1-hour lunch break.
* Core availability hours are 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM.

## 2. Leaves and Holidays
* Every employee receives 20 paid leaves per calendar year, prorated based on joining date.
* Sick leaves can be taken without prior notice, but HR must be informed by 10:00 AM.
* Leaves extending beyond 3 consecutive days require manager approval and a valid reason.
* Unused paid leaves (up to 5 days) can be carried forward to the next year.

## 3. Resignation and Notice Period
* The standard notice period for all employees is 30 days.
* Notice period starts on the day the formal resignation email is sent to HR.
* Paid leaves cannot be taken during the notice period without exceptional approval.

## 4. Payroll and Reimbursement
* Salaries are credited on the last working day of every month.
* Internet and travel reimbursements must be submitted by the 25th of the month.
* The company provides a standard health insurance cover of $50,000 for the employee.
